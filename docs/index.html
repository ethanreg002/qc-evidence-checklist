What the validator enforces

Structural checks catch contradictions in the note format. They do not turn an image or spreadsheet row into a quality result.

01

Traceable identity

Require a stable record ID, final HTTP(S) source, and exact visible selection text.

02

Evidence boundary

Keep a neutral observation separate from the question that still needs an answer.

03

Review state

Limit rows to observed, unresolved, or recheck and flag dates that are future or stale.

Local workflow

Run the checklist before manual review

  1. Export a CSV with the documented seven columns.
  2. Run tests and validate the file locally.
  3. Fix structural errors and inspect warnings.
  4. Open each source and complete the manual review checklist.

CSV contract

One row, one reviewable evidence record

FieldRequirementWhy it exists
record_idUnique and non-emptyPrevents one note from silently replacing another.
source_urlFinal HTTP(S) pageKeeps the evidence reopenable.
selection_textExact visible labelReduces size, color, version, or package mix-ups.
observationNeutral visible detailSeparates what the page shows from a broader conclusion.
unresolved_questionRequired for unresolved rowsTurns uncertainty into a specific next check.
reviewed_onISO dateDistinguishes an old note from current evidence.
stateObserved, unresolved, or recheckMakes the next action explicit.

Important limits

Validation is not inspection

The checklist does not authenticate products, score quality, verify sellers, confirm warehouse handling, or predict price, stock, shipping, or order outcomes. Those facts require current evidence from their responsible sources.
